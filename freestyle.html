What Elite Freestylers Do Differently

  • Pull starts with the catch — elbow never drops below the hand
  • Hip rotation 40–50° per side — not just shoulders
  • Kick is compact from the hip, never the knee
  • Every underwater is 10+ meters of free speed
  • DPS maximized before tempo is ever increased
  • Bilateral breathing in training; strong-side in races
The Law of Freestyle Speed
"Speed = Distance Per Stroke × Stroke Rate.
Increase DPS first. Then increase tempo.
This is how all great swimmers are made."
🎯

Core Mechanics

1 · Body Position
Foundation

Torpedo Position

Body flat, horizontal, balanced. Hips just below the surface. High head position creates massive drag — one of the biggest time thieves in high school swimming.

  • Head: Waterline splits forehead — eyes look down at 45°
  • Hips: Kick keeps them surface-level, not letting them sink
  • Chest press: Slight downward pressure on chest lifts hips
  • Streamline: Squeeze biceps to ears, hands overlapping, core engaged
2 · High Elbow Catch (EVF)
Most Important for Speed

Early Vertical Forearm

Set the catch so your forearm is nearly vertical immediately after entry — creating maximum surface area to hold water and press backward.

  • Entry: fingertips first, hand slides forward — NOT down
  • Elbow stays HIGH as forearm drops — like reaching over a barrel
  • Once forearm is vertical, press backward — not downward
  • Elbow higher than wrist, wrist higher than fingers at catch
FeelPressure on the entire palm AND forearm. If only your palm feels pressure, you're pulling — not catching.
3 · Body Rotation
Engine of Freestyle

Rotating hips and shoulders 40–50° per side reduces frontal drag and adds length and power to every stroke. Drive it from the hips — not the shoulders.

  • When right arm pulls, right hip drops — opposite arm reaches longer
  • Cue: "Slice one shoulder toward the pool bottom"
  • Rotation and kick are linked — the kick DRIVES rotation
4 · Distance Per Stroke
Efficiency Metric

strokes/25
Distance 200/500
16–20
strokes/25

Count every length. If count rises as you fatigue, technique broke down — that is your focus area.

5 · Kick Mechanics
Timing Over Power
  • Sprint: 6-beat kick — constant, fast, compact
  • Distance: 2-beat kick — one kick per arm pull, saves energy
  • Drive from the HIP — knee bend minimal (<30°)
  • Floppy ankles are a superpower — flexibility = propulsion
6 · Breathing
  • Rotate to breathe — don't lift. One goggle stays in water
  • Exhale continuously underwater — no breath-holding
  • Train bilateral (every 3); race every 2 in the 100
  • 50 free: NO breath, or one breath at the 25m wall only
7 · Underwaters
Free Speed

3–5 powerful dolphin kicks off every wall. Stay in streamline until you slow to swim speed (~7–8m from wall). This alone can drop seconds.

8 · Tempo
  • 50m: 0.9–1.1 sec per stroke cycle
  • 100m: 1.0–1.15 sec/cycle
  • 200m: 1.1–1.2 sec/cycle
  • Find optimal DPS first — then slowly increase tempo

Top 12 Drills

Rule: 90% concentration, 70% effort. Slow deliberate drills build elite mechanics faster than sloppy speed work.

Body Position
01
Superman / Float Drill

Push off in streamline, arms extended, NO kick. Focus purely on horizontal alignment.

FeelWater pressing evenly on chest and thighs. Hips float up naturally.
MistakeArching the lower back — keep core tight.
02
Chest Press + Kick

Arms at sides, kick on stomach. Press sternum slightly downward — watch hips rise.

FeelHips becoming effortlessly buoyant.
MistakeLooking forward — eyes must face DOWN.
Catch & EVF
03
Fingertip Drag

During recovery, drag fingertips along the surface from hip to entry. Forces high elbow recovery.

FeelElbow pointing up and outward.
MistakeLetting elbow drop — fingertips lose water contact.
04
Catch-Up Drill

Wait for recovering hand to "catch up" to extended hand before pulling. Trains patience at the catch.

FeelLong glide, body stays fully extended.
MistakeRushing — the whole point is deliberate slowness.
05
Fist Drill

Swim with clenched fists. Eliminates hand propulsion and forces the forearm to do the work.

FeelWater pressure on forearm, not just palm.
MistakePulling harder — slow down, focus on forearm load.
06
1-Arm (with snorkel)

One arm extended, pull with the other only. Snorkel removes breathing disruption. Alternate arms each 25.

FeelFull rotation per stroke, elbow high through the pull.
MistakeLosing balance — anchor the kick and use hip rotation.
Rotation
07
Side Kick Drill

Kick on your side — lead arm extended, top arm at hip. Alternate sides every 25.

FeelOne shoulder pointing up, body fully on its side, still moving forward.
MistakeRotating past 90° onto the back.
08
6-1-6 Drill

6 kicks on left side, 1 stroke to switch, 6 kicks on right. Connects the kick to rotation.

FeelThe single stroke causes your body to roll from the hip.
MistakeRushed switch stroke — make it one clean, full rotation.
Kick
09
Kick on Back

Flutter kick on your back, arms at sides. Watch your feet — no splash = hip kick. Big splash = knee kick.

FeelThigh muscles initiating movement, ankles loose.
MistakeFlexing ankles — floppy ankles = propulsion.
10
Vertical Kick

Tread water kick only, arms crossed on chest. 30-second intervals. Best ankle flexibility builder.

FeelRising and falling with kick tempo.
MistakeUsing arms — trust the kick alone.
Sprint Mechanics
11
DPS Target Sets

Swim 25s with a target stroke count (e.g. 16). Must not exceed it. Forces full stroke and glide.

FeelEach stroke pulling a long "rope" of water.
MistakeCheating with dolphin kick — count from 5m mark.
12
Underwater Breakout Sets

Sprint 12.5m from turn only. Goal: first stroke at or beyond the 7.5m marker.

FeelEach dolphin kick exploding from the hip.
MistakeBreaking out too early — trust the speed.

Race Strategy

Sprint · 95% Skill

50 Free — Drop Time Fast

Most time is dropped through better underwaters, higher stroke efficiency, and eliminating panic at the 15m mark.

Pacing Model

  • 0–15m: Underwater — tight streamline, 4–5 dolphin kicks
  • 15–35m: Build stroke rate, head down, max DPS
  • 35–50m: No breath — push stroke rate, kick through the flags
Quick Time Drops
  • +1 dolphin kick → saves ~0.20 sec
  • Eliminate one breath → saves ~0.35 sec
  • Reduce 2 strokes per length → saves ~0.30 sec
Sprint-Endurance

100 Free — Split Strategy

Goal: hold the same stroke count on lengths 3 & 4 as lengths 1 & 2. If it rises more than 2, you went out too fast.

Split Formula

Goal time × 0.47 = ideal first 50 split.
55.0 goal → 25.85 first 50.
Going out in 24.5 means you blow up.

Pacing Model

  • First 50: Controlled explosive — not a sprint
  • Third 25: Mental fight — hold technique, rate climbs
  • Last 25: No breathing, max kick, lengthen strokes
Aerobic Sprint

200 Free — Even Split

The 200 rewards patience. Going 2 seconds too fast on the first 50 costs 4–6 seconds on the back half.

4-Split Model

  • 1st 50: Relaxed, long — feel good pace
  • 2nd 50: Lock in rhythm, no acceleration yet
  • 3rd 50: Build — stroke rate rises
  • 4th 50: Empty the tank
Distance Event

500 Free — Think Metronome

Won in the middle 300. Lock in your stroke count on lap 2 and HOLD it through lap 9. Kick up to 6-beat for last 2 laps.

Warning Sign

Stroke count rising more than 2 per lap as race progresses = went out too fast or technique broke. Train holding count in 200–400m sets.

Race-Day Checklist
  • ✓
    Night before: Visualize your race 3 times. See your perfect underwater and first stroke.
  • ✓
    Warm-up: 800m minimum — drills, race-pace swims, underwaters.
  • ✓
    10 min before: 4–6 fast 25s, last one at full race pace.
  • ✓
    On the block: 3–4 slow deep breaths. Visualize breakout. Relax shoulders.
  • ✓
    Post-race: 200–400m easy, note stroke count and what broke down.

Top 10 Mistakes

Mistake 1

Head Too High

Looking forward sinks your hips — creates drag equal to a parachute. Eyes face DOWN, waterline splits the forehead.

FixChest press kick drill + underwater video of head position.
Mistake 2

Crossover Entry

Hand crossing the centerline causes your body to snake and kills rotation balance. Enter at shoulder-width.

Fix1-arm drill + front-angle video of hand entry.
Mistake 3

Dropped Elbow at Catch

Elbow dropping before the forearm pushes water DOWN not backward. Loses power, risks shoulder injury.

FixFist drill + catch-up drill. "Elbow high, forearm drops like a shelf."
Mistake 4

Lifting Head to Breathe

Every head lift drops your hips. The bow wave creates a natural trough — rotate into it.

FixSnorkel practice, then reintroduce breathing via rotation-only.
Mistake 5

Bicycle Kick

Large knee bend creates drag and loses propulsion. Drive from the hip, whip through the ankle.

FixBack kick + vertical kick sets.
Mistake 6

Early Pull Exit

Finishing pull at hip instead of thigh loses 25% of the push phase. Thumb must brush the thigh before exit.

Mistake 7

Weak Underwaters

Breaking out before 7m leaves free speed untouched. Every weak streamline = half-second lost per wall.

Fix12.5m push-off sets, 3× per session.
Mistake 8

Hyperventilating on the Block

Over-breathing depletes CO2 — your breathe signal. Take 3–4 slow deep breaths, not 10 fast ones.

Mistake 9

Kick Dies in the Back Half

When kick stops, hips sink, drag surges, race is over. Kick maintains position — not just propulsion.

FixKick-sprint sets: kickboard sprint 25s between swim 50s.
Mistake 10

Going Out Too Fast

2 seconds too fast on the first 50 costs 4–6 on the back half. Even splits win. Negative splits win championships.
